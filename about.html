About RExplorer

A free, read-only map for exploring land, homes and commercial property in Clarksville and Montgomery County, Tennessee.

Who it is for

  • Home buyers and builders looking for a house or a lot, with flood zones, wetlands and elevation on the map.
  • Real estate agents who want parcel facts, sales history, comparable sales and active listings in one place.
  • Investors and developers screening sites by zoning, size, traffic, people and jobs nearby, with a suitability score for common uses.
  • Large land buyers comparing tracts and reading neighboring parcels together as one site.

Where the data comes from

Every field in a parcel's details names its source and date. Parcels and zoning come from the Montgomery County Assessor of Property and the APSU GIS Center, shown with their permission. Flood zones (FEMA), wetlands (USFWS), city limits (U.S. Census), traffic counts and road projects (TDOT), elevation (USGS 3DEP via Esri), demographics (U.S. Census) and businesses (Overture Maps) are shown with credit to each. The full list is under About and data in the map.

Privacy

No account is needed and nothing is tracked. Owner names and mailing addresses are never collected. Favorites and comparisons are kept in your own browser.

For research only. RExplorer is not a survey, title search, appraisal or flood determination; confirm anything important with the county, a surveyor or a licensed professional.
