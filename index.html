A free, read-only map for exploring commercial property in Clarksville and Montgomery County, Tennessee: parcels, zoning, flood zones and nearby businesses, each with its source and date. No account, no tracking, no downloads. Owner names and mailing addresses are never collected.

For research only. It is not a survey, title search, appraisal or flood determination; confirm anything important with the county or the business.

This is an early preview. Parcel data is not shown yet while permission from the data owners is pending.

  • Flood hazard zones: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, displayed live from FEMA's public map service. For information only; not an official flood determination.
  • Parcels and zoning: Montgomery County Assessor of Property and APSU GIS Center, shown with their permission for this personal, non-commercial project. Downloads are not offered. Owner names and mailing addresses are never collected.
  • Businesses and places: Overture Maps Foundation and its contributors (Meta, Microsoft, Foursquare, AllThePlaces and others), under CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Apache-2.0 or CC0-1.0 per record.
  • Basemap: Esri and its data providers (see map attribution).
  • Businesses and places: Overture Maps Foundation and its contributors (CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Apache-2.0 or CC0-1.0 per record).
  • Buildings on each parcel: Overture Maps Foundation (ODbL 1.0); © OpenStreetMap contributors, Microsoft, Google Open Buildings, Esri Community Maps.
  • Basemap: Esri and its data providers. 3D buildings: Esri 3D Buildings (TomTom, Vantor, Esri Community Maps, OpenStreetMap contributors, Microsoft, Google Open Buildings, USGS).
  • Flood zones: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer. Wetlands: USFWS National Wetlands Inventory. City limits: U.S. Census TIGERweb. Road projects: TDOT.
  • Business icons: Lucide (ISC license).
